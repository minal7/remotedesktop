# Assemble source-reported options across observed detail pages

Source checkpoint, 2026-09-08. Previous checkpoint: `f73f646`. Execution remains deferred.

## First-principles defect

A request for several options often requires several detail pages. The preceding answer implementation required every selected option to appear in the current reading. It could retain earlier pages as context, but could not include their candidates in the final answer. Consequently, two individually supported options on different pages could never satisfy a two-option request.

Simply concatenating the archive would introduce different errors. An earlier page may belong to an older goal or account context. A later reading of the same URL may contradict it. The same concert can appear at multiple URLs. Stock observed earlier must not be presented as a new live check. The answer must preserve these distinctions and the qualifications supporting each selected option.

A newer recorded reading of the same exact native URL supersedes an older one, even if the newer reading has no eligible results. A current reading also excludes older observations of its URL. URL comparisons remain native; model context receives reading indices, not reconstructed destinations.
- Manual control invalidates all existing archive entries for terminal answer use. Their complete observations remain available for context, and new readings after explicit handback can supply answers again. This avoids assuming that unchanged user wording means an unchanged account or selection after login. The archive digest includes this boundary, so an earlier selection cannot survive it. Goal clarification likewise retains older context without promoting its old assessment into evidence for the new goal.
- Catalogs identify observation time, a newer reading index when one exists, and whether fresh evidence is required after manual control. These are provenance hints, not semantic eligibility. They let the planner identify why an old reading cannot supply an answer and which later reading needs inspection.
- [Answer eligibility](../../../host-mac/RemoteDesktopHost/ComputerUse/ComputerUseBrowserResearchAnswer.swift) separates per-page candidate admission from combined selection. Each contributing candidate still needs complete individual support under the existing requirement ledger and scalar checks. This repair combines complete options; it does not infer that facts from separate incomplete candidates describe the same offer or merge their missing facts.
- The answer option table maps each offered answer index to a current or fully recalled reading and that reading's candidate index. Current navigation indices and answer indices remain separate namespaces. Up to eight selected options can satisfy the original result-count predicates across the combined pool. Exact duplicate normalized whole passages cannot count twice, including across different URLs; semantic duplicates with different wording still require model interpretation.
- [The local planner](../../../host-mac/RemoteDesktopHost/ComputerUse/ComputerUseBrowserLinkPlanner.swift) receives decision input version 7 and schema `browser_result_link_v4`. It must recall a historical reading in full before choosing its options, consider all original requirements and complete qualifications, and reject incompatible dates, accounts, selections or offer types. Existing eight-recall/nine-call bounds, exact token counting and the common 30-second decision deadline remain in force; an answer does not bypass a pending navigation effect.
- [Owner selection and preparation](../../../host-mac/RemoteDesktopHost/ComputerUse/ComputerUseBrowserResearchTask.swift) reconstruct the exact combined eligibility using the retained archive and ordered recall set. Changed readings, archive state, goal, policy or control ownership invalidate the proposal. Preparation revalidates the current native page before and after rendering. Historical observations remain explicitly historical; preparation does not reopen or claim to revalidate all earlier websites.
- Each delivered option includes its exact permitted source URL, immutable UTC observation time and complete quoted passage. All surrounding text from every selected reading is included once, along with the full original requirement sources. The response warns that availability can change after observation. Existing 4,000-Character/16-KiB limits reject oversized complete messages rather than truncating qualifications. Replaying a durable answer preserves its original observation times and does not perform another search or inference.

The current page must still be eligible for complete reading context. The previous restrictions on omitted controls/structure/content, unsupported conditions, quantity policies and reportable URLs remain. Query- or fragment-bearing source URLs remain unreportable. This is a source-reported informational answer, not a booking receipt, a fresh stock guarantee or independent verification of semantic matching.
